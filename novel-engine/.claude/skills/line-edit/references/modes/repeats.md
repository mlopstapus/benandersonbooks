# Mode: repeats

Chapters drafted one at a time repeat each other: the same beat (a second identical catch), the same image, the same joke, the same description of a place, and recaps of things the reader already knows.

1. Build a list, per chapter, of its distinctive beats, images, lines and descriptions (the details ledger in `story/continuity.md` helps).
2. Read in order and flag:
   - a beat or image that repeats an earlier one without acknowledging it;
   - a recap of events the reader saw (one short reminder after a long gap is fine; a paragraph is not);
   - a character re-introduced with the same description;
   - a phrase or line that echoes too closely (especially dialogue in a late chapter that replays an early scene).
3. For deliberate callbacks, keep them and make the callback clear. For accidental ones, propose a cut or a change.

All findings are taste calls unless the repeat is a plain duplicate (the same sentence twice).
