# Mode: word sweep

For a change that must be made everywhere: a term the reader won't know, a renamed thing, a phrase the author dislikes, a rule that changed what something is called.

1. Search every chapter and canon page for the word and its variants (plurals, capitalized, inside other words). Read each hit in context; a sweep is never a blind find-and-replace.
2. For each hit choose the replacement that fits the sentence (the same word won't fit everywhere: in *Ash of Verdance* "the bench" for a court became "the magistrate", "the hearing" or "court", depending on the line). Check rhymes, wordplay and dialogue rhythm.
3. Update canon pages and idioms-and-terms with the new term, and add the old one to the style guide's reader's-English rule (or section I) so it doesn't come back.
4. Report the count per chapter and any hits deliberately kept.
