# Stage 3: cast

One character per round. Main characters first (protagonist, antagonist, the one or two closest supporting characters), then supporting, then a list of minor roles.

## For each main or supporting character
Use `templates/pages/character.md`. Work through:
- Snapshot: age, look (two or three markers a reader will notice), job, home.
- Want, need, wound; why they can't have it yet; their personal connection to the plot.
- **Arc from start to end**, matching the ending in the outline.
- Backstory: locked facts only. **Name the off-page people who matter** (a dead spouse, a parent, a mentor). In *Ash of Verdance* one such character had no name until chapter 21, when the book's second-biggest scene needed one.
- Voice: diction, where their images come from, how they argue, two or three sample lines. Decide who is terse and who isn't (style guide B13).
- How they use or are affected by the core system.
- **Knows / doesn't know** at the start: every secret in the book, from this character's side.
- Relationships to the rest of the cast.
- One or two quirks that can carry humor or texture (a rhyming habit, a weakness for pastries).

Supporting characters can be shorter, but always get motive, voice and knows/doesn't know.

## Minor roles
A table in `story/world/idioms-and-terms.md` (name, role, where they appear). Name them deliberately; never accept model-default names (see `craft/ai-workflow/ai-prose-tells.md`).

## Close
Check the cast against `craft/scope.md`. Commit `plan: cast`.
