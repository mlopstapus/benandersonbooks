# {{Core system}}

The thing in this book that must work by rules the reader can trust. For fantasy it is the magic; for sci-fi the technology; for mystery the crime; for historical fiction the period's hard constraints. The genre module (templates/genres/) supplies the specific questions; this page holds the answers.
