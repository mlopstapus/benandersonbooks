# Series Arc

The plan across every book in the series. Each book's detailed plan lives in its own [[outline]]; settled lines also appear in [[decisions]]. Facts about one character, institution or place live on that page; this page tracks how the threads move book by book.

## Shape
One line per book: **the question it asks**, the focus lead (if focus rotates), what it wins and what that win costs or leaves open.
- **Book 1: <question>?** Focus: … Ends with …
- **Book 2: <question>?** Focus: … Ends with …
- **Book 3: <question>?** Focus: … Ends with …

## Series threads
Each thread is **hinted** in an early book, **turns** in a middle book and **pays off** in the last. Specific hints are tracked in each book's clue and payoff table.

### 1. <thread name>
- What it is, in two lines.
- Book 1: … · Book 2: … · Book 3: …

### The antagonist, layer by layer
A strong series antagonist (especially an institution) has motives that are revealed one layer per book: a **sincere layer** the reader half agrees with, a **self-interest layer** (power, money, survival), and a **buried crime or secret**. Name a character who embodies the sincere layer, so it is a person, not a position.
| Book | Layer revealed | How the leads learn it | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|

- **Their lies:** what the antagonist tells everyone that isn't true, and the book where each lie cracks.
- **Their muscle:** who enforces for them (honestly or not), and whether and when that force turns, with the trigger.

### Escalation ladder
What gets bigger each book: the breakthroughs the heroes make, the force used against them, the stakes. Each step should threaten someone new.
| Book | Breakthrough or step | How the heroes get it | What it threatens | The force against them |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## The endgame and its cost
What the last book's final choice is, what it costs each lead personally, and how it answers the series' opening image or prologue.

## Theme
The question underneath (leaning or canon), and how each side of it is made human.

## The leads
One line each, linking their pages: who they are, their focus book, their relationship to the truth (each lead should handle truth differently).

### How they meet (book 1)
Why their goals tie together. Each lead should want something only another can give.

## Handoffs
What each book leaves for the next: the open question, the object or place that is the way back in, the goal the next book starts with.
