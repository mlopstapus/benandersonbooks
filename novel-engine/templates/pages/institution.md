# {{In-world name}} ({{plain role}})

One page per guild, house, agency, church, company or faction. Facts involving two or more institutions go on the hub ([[institutions]] or the book's named hub page).

## Name
In-world name, where it comes from, what members are called (and any street name). Checked against [[names]].

## Represents
What part of society it stands for, in a line.

## What they do
Their daily work, and what the rest of society depends on them for.

## Power
What gives them leverage (a resource, knowledge, force, law), what they control because of it, and their blind spot.

## Head and named members
Name, role, weight (main or walk-on), page link. Walk-on sketches stay proposed until approved.

## Their lies and secrets (if any)
What they tell the public that isn't true, what only an inner circle knows, and which book or chapter reveals each.

## Motives
What they want, at each level (members, leadership, inner circle). For the antagonist institution, which layer each book reveals (see [[series-arc]] or the outline).

## Relationships
One line per other institution, from this side. The full two-sided relationship lives on the hub.
