The reference sample for the book's prose voice. Read it with [[style-guide]] before drafting any chapter. Anything invented for the test (the anchor, the setting, side characters) is a placeholder, not canon, unless the author promotes it.

## What makes it work (for drafting)
- **The book's voice:** sentence rhythm, paragraph length, how much description, where the humor comes from.
- **This character in particular:** how their attitude shows in the narration, not only in dialogue.
- How competence and the core system show on the page.
- How the cost of the system shows without a lecture.
