# Outline: {{TITLE}}

Written ending-first. `book-plan` builds it; `write-chapter` reads one row at a time; canon sync updates rows when an approved chapter changes the plan.

# Promises
What the first chapters promise the reader (tone, the kind of story, the central question). Each must be paid off.

# Premise
Two sentences: who wants what, what stands in the way, what it will cost.

# Plot threads (end state first)
For each thread: where it ends, then the steps that get it there, chapter by chapter.

## A. <main thread>: ends with …

# Antagonist plan (off the page)
What the antagonist does each day regardless of the hero, what they know when, and why they escalate.

# Clue and payoff plan
| Setup / clue | Planted in | Points to | Paid off in | Reader should suspect by |
|---|---|---|---|---|

# Set pieces
The climax and the 3–5 emotional scenes the book exists for. For each: who is there, what happens in substance, what it pays off, which locked rules it uses.

# Climax
Beat by beat, using only locked rules.

# Ending and denouement
End state of every thread and every main character's arc. Epilogue, if any.
