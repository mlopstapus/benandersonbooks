# Decisions

A ledger of the author's decisions. One line each: the decision, the date, its scope (this book, the series, the whole world) and a link to the page where it lives in full. Don't restate the page here; when a decision replaces an earlier one, say so in the line ("replaces …").

## Settled
- Example: Point of view is close third, one POV per chapter (replaces omniscient), YYYY-MM-DD, series ([[style-guide]]).

## Open
One line per open question: `- [ ] question (raised YYYY-MM-DD, affects ChNN / book N / system)`. Questions the author has said can wait stay here until the chapter or book that needs them. When settled, move it to Settled as a one-liner with the answer and date.
