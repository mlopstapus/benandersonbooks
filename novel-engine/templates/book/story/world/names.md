## Do not use
- **From the author's earlier books** (`off_limits` in `book.yaml`): names and near-matches (same first name, same surname, sound-alikes).
- **Famous series in the same genre:** names and coined terms readers would recognize (check a proposed name for the system, the cataclysm or a place against the model authors' books).
- **AI-default names:** see [[ai-prose-tells]] (Elara, Kai, Luna, Zephyr, Voss and similar), plus any the author adds.
