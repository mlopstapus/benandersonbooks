- A scene brief is the chapter's blueprint: character objectives, stakes, emotional arc, intended reader response at the end.
- Set up context first: story type statement, tone, story norms (chapter length, POV), recurring elements.
- Use the same platform for brief and draft. Briefs keep pacing tight, prevent filler, and make editing easier.
