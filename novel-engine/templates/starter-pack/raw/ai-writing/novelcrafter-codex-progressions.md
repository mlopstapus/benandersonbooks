# Source note — Novelcrafter docs: Codex Progressions/Additions
URL: https://www.novelcrafter.com/help/docs/codex/progressions-additions
Retrieved: 2026-10-01 via web fetch (summary extraction, not verbatim).

- The Codex is a story bible (characters, locations, lore) that the AI references when generating.
- **Progressions** are dated addenda to codex entries that the AI only "sees" when working on scenes at/after that point in the story — prevents anachronisms and spoilers (e.g., a character's marriage or injury showing up in earlier scenes).
- Use for lasting changes (transformations, damaged locations); not for individual plot points (put those in scene instructions).
