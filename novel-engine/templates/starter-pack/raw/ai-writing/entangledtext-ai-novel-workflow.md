# Source note — "AI Novel Writing Workflow: Outline → Draft → Edit → KDP (2026)"
URL: https://www.entangledtext.com/ai-novel-writing-workflow
Retrieved: 2026-10-01 via web fetch (summary extraction, not verbatim). Note: vendor blog (promotes its own tool).

- Stage 1 Lock the premise: who wants what, why now, cost of failure; want vs need; antagonistic force; escalating stakes; hard constraints (magic costs etc.). "If you cannot state the conflict in two sentences, AI will invent a new book every chapter."
- Stage 2 Story spine: beats (three-act / Save the Cat) → chapter rows with POV, goal, conflict, outcome. Test: "what changes by the end of chapter N?"
- Stage 3 Living story bible, compact: voice samples (2–3 lines per character), physical markers, secrets each POV can't access yet, open threads. Update after chapters that add facts.
- Stage 4 Draft chapter-by-chapter with tight briefs: job (goal/conflict/outcome/length), previous-chapter summary (<200 words), only relevant bible notes, explicit must-include / must-not. Read every chapter before generating the next — continuity debt compounds.
- Stage 5 Review with diffs, accept/reject changes deliberately; never regenerate the whole book unreviewed.
- Stage 6 Revision passes in fixed order: structure → continuity → scene craft → line edit → proofread. "Line editing before structure is a common AI-era trap."
- Stage 7 Beta readers with targeted questions ("Where did you skim?").
- Four-block prompt: Job / Must include / Must not / Context pack.
- Continuity gates every 5–8 chapters (names, timeline, knowledge states). Treat AI-invented subplots as brief failure, not bonus. Update the outline when the model deviates usefully.
- Fantasy guardrail: encode magic costs as constraints in every prompt. Thriller: track reader vs POV knowledge (AI collapses dramatic irony).
