# What *Ash of Verdance*'s readers taught the engine

*Ash of Verdance*, the author's previous novel, launched 2026-11-17. The first reader reviews (2026-11-16) praised its costly magic, its family heart, the support-group scenes, the warm ensemble, the fair-play mystery and the hero/villain mirror. They criticized the repetition of the magic's ritual (about 134 mentions of one ritual, roughly five a chapter), over-explaining, world markers that arrived late (a real-world holiday), too many place names, and the pacing after the reveal.

Every one of these reached the author only in published reviews. The changes below move each catch earlier: into planning, the chapter check, a cold read of the opening, and advance readers before the final lock. *Thornwake* applied them book-side first (its outline → Reader lessons and Motif budget, style guide B53–B54, present-day → World markers).

| Readers flagged | Where it lives now |
|---|---|
| The magic's ritual and its sensations described again and again; low word-for-word duplication, so the recurring beat was the problem, not copied sentences | Style guide B53 (motif budget) and its self-check line; lock-rules §1 lists every recurring ritual and sensation; outline stage §6 item 6 and the outline template's **Motif budget** table; brief template **Motifs** line; check stage's **Repetition count** (motif density per chapter against the budget, flagging chapters above the running average) |
| Too many place names; streets blurred together | Style guide B54 (one-trait places) and its self-check line; world stage round 7: name only recurring places, one trait each, a cap in `craft/scope.md` |
| World markers set late (a real-world holiday) | World stage round 3: a **World markers** section on the present-day page (calendar, religion, holidays, real-world references, oaths, technology), planted in chapters 1–3 |
| Over-explaining; confusing terms a first-time reader can't place | **Cold read of chapters 1–3** in write-chapter's approve stage, before chapter 4's brief: a fresh subagent with no wiki, and one human reader, flag only confusing terms and missing setting cues. `write-chapter` and `book-next` offer it when chapter 3 is synced and no report exists |
| Pacing after the reveal | Outline stage §6 item 5: every chapter after the big reveal turns something, or merges with its neighbor |
| All of the above found only after release | **Advance readers before the final lock** in line-edit's pre-release pass and book-next's Revising row, while changes are still cheap |

## Keep
What readers loved is already the engine's default: costs shown on specific people (A5), the spine as a mirror (book-plan spine stage), the clue and payoff plan (fair play), and recurring sets (`sets.md`) for warm communal scenes. No change; listed so a later trim doesn't cut them.

## Still untested
- The repetition count's thresholds (above the running average, the budget's caps) haven't been run on a real chapter yet; *Thornwake* chapter 3 will be the first.
- Whether a subagent cold reader finds the same things a human one does.
