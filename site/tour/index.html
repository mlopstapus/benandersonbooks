A NOTE ON THE DRAWING
Built from the page.
This tour imagines the Hale house near the start of Ash of Verdance. It uses the novel’s physical descriptions without retelling what happens in its rooms.
What comes from the book
  • Chapter 2, Mags: number six, the narrow front parlor, the kitchen table, lamp, and satchel peg by the back door.
  • Chapter 8, Sallow Jack: Gideon’s washstand and looking glass, and Callum’s coat on its hook.
  • Chapter 7, The Docks: the back gate, the kitchen range, and the clock on its shelf.
  • Chapter 12, Seized: the hallway, creaking third stair, parlor mantel and clock, and the ill-fitting sash in Callum’s back bedroom.
  • Chapters 13–15: the iron back latch, stone threshold, flagstones, range fender, kitchen cupboard, bread crock, coal scuttles, jug, and cup.
  • Chapters 19, 25 & 26: the back lane, yard gate, kettle and rail, old pencil marks on the kitchen door, and the front window’s aged lace. Only descriptions of objects are used here.
  • World notes, Ashcombe: a two-up two-down terrace on Wicker Street, roughly ten minutes from the bridge.
What the illustration fills in
The exact dimensions, room arrangement, window positions, roof, brickwork, furniture shapes, street paving, and yard planting are artistic choices. The settee, Gideon’s dresser, and the arrangement of domestic objects fill in the house around its described furnishings. The rug is set back to leave the significant hearth boards visible. In this tour, the back entrance is placed at the end of the hall, beside the kitchen. Neighbouring houses and the street lamp establish the setting. The marker on the city map is approximate.
The tour is a three-dimensional ink drawing. The house opens as a cutaway overview. Step inside to look around at eye level, click the floor rings to move, or follow an arrow through an open doorway. The rooms share one connected hall, staircase and landing; moving between them follows that route. Numbered viewpoint buttons offer the same route. Choose Overview to rotate a cutaway of the room. You can also use the arrow keys while the scene has focus, or select each detail in the notes. The parlor, kitchen, and both bedrooms can all be entered; the street and yard connect them.
